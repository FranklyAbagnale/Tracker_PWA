Plate states

Every plate has a state. A plate is identified by state + plate, so CA-ABC1234 and NV-ABC1234 are different plates. US and Canadian codes are 2 letters, Mexican states are 3 letters (ISO 3166-2:MX). USG and GOV are federal and government plates and can carry a code after a dash, for example GOV-DHS or USG-G42; the code is part of the state. Searching GOV finds every GOV-… plate.
