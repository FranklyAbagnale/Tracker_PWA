## Prior art and related tools
- Jotform License Plate Tracking App — no-code template. Useful as a
  field-UI reference for what information matters when logging a vehicle.
  Not suitable as the backend.
- OpenALPR / Rekor — commercial ALPR. Different problem (automatic
  recognition from camera feeds), but worth knowing the commercial feature
  set.
- Plate Recognizer — API-based ALPR with a free tier. Could later automate
  plate entry from a photo, replacing manual typing.
- Decap CMS / Netlify CMS / Tina — Git-backed content editing. Their write
  paths and conflict handling are instructive.
