# ParkingTracker: Tester Guide

Thanks for helping test. This takes about 10 minutes.

## Read this first
**This is a test. Everything you save is publicly visible on the internet,
including photos.** Use test data only:

- Do **not** photograph real people, real license plates, or real addresses.
  Use a toy car, a printed picture, a screen, or a blank wall.
- Invent plates. Anything starting with `TEST` is ideal (`TEST123`).
- Start your notes with `TEST`.

## Set up
1. Open the link you were sent on your phone (iPhone: Safari; Android: Chrome).
2. **Add to Home Screen** (iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: menu, then Install app).
3. Open it from the new icon.
4. Enter **your name** (it is saved with everything you add) and the **access
   token** you were sent separately. Tap **Sign in**.

5. On the home screen tap **Allow** when asked about location and camera, then
   allow each prompt. The app only uses them while it is open. If you tap Not
   now or block them, the app still works: type coordinates for Local, and use
   "Choose or take a photo" instead of the live camera.

Tap the **Tracker logo** at any time to return home. The **back arrow** left of the pin takes you to the last screen you viewed; it is not shown when the app first opens. The three-line menu (top
right) has Add vehicle (no photo), Tutorial, Glossary, Sync now and Log out.

Keep the token private. Do not post it anywhere.

## What to try
There are 16 pretend vehicles around Pasadena. If you are elsewhere, use
**Local -> Enter coordinates**: `34.1478`, `-118.1445`.

1. **Local**: tap Use my location, or enter the coordinates above. You should see
   a ranked list. Tap one to see its photos and history.
2. **Plate**: search `TST1001`. Then search `TST1O01` (letter O): you should get a suggestion.
3. **Photo**: capture and accept two photos of something harmless, then Done.
4. **Vehicle**: fill in the details, plate `TEST` plus three digits, and Save.
   It should say saved, then "All synced" on the home screen.
5. **Plate** again: look up your new plate, change the flag, add a note, Save update.
6. **Offline**: turn on airplane mode, save another vehicle. The home screen
   should say it is waiting. Turn airplane mode off; it should upload by itself.

The full checklist is `Phone Test with Sample Data.md` if you want to go further.

## If something goes wrong
Please send:
- what you tapped, and what you expected
- what happened instead (a screenshot helps)
- your phone model and browser, and roughly what time it was
- the red or amber message on screen, word for word

Do not enter the token again on other websites. If the app says "Token
rejected", the token was changed; ask for the new one.
