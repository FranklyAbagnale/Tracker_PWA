
1. **Open** https://franklyabagnale.github.io/ParkingTracker/ in your phone browser (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android).
2. **Add to home screen.**
    - iPhone: Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
    - Android: ⋮ menu → **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
    - Open Parky from the new icon from now on.
3. **Sign in.** Enter your name (so your sightings are labelled) and the access token you were given (`github_pat_…`), then tap **Sign in**. Don't share the token. Allow camera and location when asked.

**Home screen**

- 📷 **Photo**: take photos of a vehicle. **Capture** takes one, **Accept** keeps it, **Retake** redoes it, and **Add photos** adds more. **Done** saves.
- 🔍 **Plate**: type a plate, or any 3 or more characters of one, and tap **Search**.
    - Leave state on **Any state**. If the matches span several states, tap a state button to narrow the list.
    - Tap a result to open the vehicle and add a sighting.
    - If nothing matches, **Create new vehicle with this plate** adds it.
- 🚗 **Vehicle**: add a vehicle by hand (plate, state, color, make, model, features, notes), then **Save vehicle**.
- 📍 **Local**: find vehicles seen near a place. Tap **Use my location**, or enter coordinates, then **Search**.

**Menu (☰, top right)**

- **Add vehicle (no photo)**: opens the vehicle form without taking a photo.
- **Tutorial**: opens the screenshot walkthrough of the app in a new tab.
- **Glossary**: opens a page that explains every abbreviation: state codes (US, Canada, Mexico, `USG`, `GOV`), flags, plate types and terms. It has a filter box.
- **Sync now**: sends anything saved while you were offline.
- **Retry failed**: appears only if an upload failed. Tap it to try again.
- **Log out**: removes your name and token from this phone.

**Other buttons**

- **↻ Refresh index**: reloads the vehicle list. Use it if a plate someone just added doesn't show up. The list is cached for 24 hours.
- **Save update**: saves changes when you edit an existing vehicle.
- **Cancel**: goes back to the home screen.
- Tapping the logo also returns home.

**If something's off:** the app works offline and syncs later, so a red badge on ☰ means there are items waiting to upload. Open the menu and tap **Sync now**.
